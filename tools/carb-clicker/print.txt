Carb click screwdriver — bench only. Do not leave it on the car.

Print three STLs. PETG if you can. 5 walls, 40% infill.
  shaft.stl   standing, Phillips tip on the bed
  handle.stl  wings flat on the bed
  collar.stl  standing — this is the click ring you hold

Hardware: M3×12 and an M3 nut through the handle.

Use
  Seat the screw. Hold the collar still. Turn the butterfly.
  You will see the 12-tooth gear jump one tooth per click.

  12 clicks = 1 turn ≈ 0.8 mm
   3 = 1/4 turn
   6 = 1/2 turn
  15 = 1-1/4
  21 = 1-3/4   ← current mixture on both carbs
