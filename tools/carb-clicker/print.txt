Carb measuring driver — bench only. Do not leave it on the car.

Two prints
  dial.stl     numbered ring with two click pawls. Flat on the bed.
  driver.stl   wishbone, 12-tooth gear, arrow. Stand it up, hex on the bed.
               5 walls, 40% infill. PETG. Total height 2 in.

Bit
  Milwaukee 1/4 in hex insert bit (Shockwave or any kit bit).
  Push it into the bottom. Optional M3 set screw to pinch it.

Use
  Slide the ring onto the driver from the hex end until it pops over the chamfer
  and the gear sits in the hole. Two gray fingers (at 12 and 6) drop into
  gaps between teeth — those are the clicks.
  Seat the bit on the screw. Hold the numbered ring.
  Turn the wishbone. You will see and feel each click.
  One click = one number. 12 clicks = one full turn.
  If the ring slips in your hand, it will not click — do not count that.
  Same click count on both carbs.
