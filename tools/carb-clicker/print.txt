Carb measuring driver — bench only. Do not leave it on the car.

Two prints
  dial.stl     the numbered ring (1–12). Flat on the bed.
  driver.stl   butterfly, pointer, shaft. Stand it up, hex on the bed.
               5 walls, 40% infill. Total height 2 in.

Bit
  Milwaukee 1/4 in hex insert bit (Shockwave or any kit bit).
  Push it into the bottom. Optional M3 set screw to pinch it.
  Use slotted or Phillips — whatever matches the carb screw.

Use
  Drop the dial onto the driver so the pointer sits in the hole.
  Seat the screw. Hold the numbered ring still.
  Turn the butterfly. The arrow points at 1–12.
  Same number on both carbs. 12 clicks around = one full turn.
