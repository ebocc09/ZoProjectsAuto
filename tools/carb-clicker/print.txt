Carb measuring driver — bench only. Do not leave it on the car.

Three prints
  dial.stl     mm ring with two click pawls. Flat on the bed.
  driver.stl   shaft + 10-tooth gear. Stand it up, hex on the bed.
  handle.stl   flat twist bar. Flat on the bed — no round bubbles.
               PETG, 5 walls, 40% infill.

Hardware
  Milwaukee 1/4 in hex insert bit in the bottom.
  One M3×12 screw. Optional M3 nut in the handle pocket.

Assemble
  1. Drop the ring onto the shaft from the top. The gear sits in the hole,
     the two fingers click into gaps between teeth.
  2. Push the handle onto the D-shaped post. It only fits one way.
     The arrow points at 0.
  3. M3 through the handle into the post. That traps the ring.

Scale
  One full turn of the carb screw is 1 mm.
  10 teeth around the gear. One click = 0.1 mm.
  Ten clicks = 1.0 mm = back to 0.
  Same click count on both carbs.
  If the ring slips, it does not click — do not count that.
