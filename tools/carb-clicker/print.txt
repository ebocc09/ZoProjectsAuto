Carb click screwdriver — bench only. Do not leave it on the car.

Print three STLs. PETG if you can (collar pawl). Else PLA.
  shaft.stl   standing, bit-boss on the bed. 5 walls.
  handle.stl  flat, wings on the bed. 5 walls.
  collar.stl  standing, pointer up.

Hardware
  1/4 in hex bit — slotted 4–5 mm for these S&S screws, or PH1
  M3×12 and an M3 nut (handle to shaft)
  Optional M3×6 set screw to pinch the bit

Use
  Seat the screw softly. Hold the collar. Turn the numbered butterfly.
  Each click is one step. 360 degrees, no stop.

  12 clicks = 1 turn ≈ 0.8 mm
   3 clicks = 1/4 turn
   6 clicks = 1/2 turn
  15 clicks = 1-1/4
  21 clicks = 1-3/4   ← current mixture on both carbs

  Same click count on left and right. That is the whole point.
